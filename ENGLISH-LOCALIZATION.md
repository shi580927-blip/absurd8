# English localization for Yandex Games

## Store listing

**Title**

The Cat Who Lives Too Well

**Short description**

Feed Chef and help this spoiled orange cat conquer the Universe!

**SEO description**

Feed Chef, hire helpers, unlock outfits and rewards, and turn a spoiled orange cat into the master of the entire Universe.

**Full description**

Chef is an orange cat with an impeccable appetite and rapidly growing demands. Feed him, collect fish, purchase increasingly absurd privileges, and watch an ordinary house cat become the master of an apartment, the Moon, and the entire Universe.

Hire a feeder grandma, a terrified cook, and lightspeed delivery. Unlock outfits and awards, look after Chef’s mood, and assemble a luxurious banquet right on the carpet. A full cat may occasionally change his mind — management decisions are final.

**How to play**

Tap Chef or press the Feed button to earn fish. Spend them in the Privileges section. Purchases increase the amount earned per tap or the number of fish received every second.

Use the button with Chef’s face to check his requests, buy dishes, and unlock new outfits, levels, and awards. You can voluntarily watch a rewarded ad to receive an instant supply of fish, triple your income temporarily, or unlock a selected dish. Rewards are granted only after the full video has been watched.

Progress is saved automatically.

cat, clicker, cat simulator, idle, humor, pet, feeding, upgrades, casual game
